About

About Delixio

Delixio is an AI-powered recipe app for iPhone and Android. It turns ingredients you already have into practical meal ideas and full recipes, then helps you save, shop, and plan.

The problem it solves

Many people open the fridge and struggle to decide what to cook. Ingredients sit unused, dinners default to the same few meals, and food gets wasted. Delixio starts from what you already have so cooking decisions are faster and more practical. If that sounds familiar, read what can I cook with these ingredients, or explore the fridge recipe app approach. To waste less food, see reduce food waste and use ingredients before they expire.

How ingredient-based recipes work

You type the ingredients you want to use, set preferences (cooking time, servings, meal type, diet, dislikes), and choose Strict, Flexible, or Creative mode. Delixio generates free recipe ideas around those ingredients. Unlock a full recipe when you want the complete version (1 credit). You can save recipes to lists, build a grocery list, plan meals on the calendar, and optionally export plans one-way to your device calendar.

For a step-by-step walkthrough, see How it works. For ingredient-first meal ideas, see recipe generator from ingredients.

Available on iPhone and Android

Delixio is currently available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. You can also open the download page.

Main capabilities

  • AI recipe ideas from ingredients you already have (ideas are free)
  • Full recipes unlocked with credits (1 credit = 1 full recipe)
  • Strict, Flexible, and Creative cooking modes
  • Grocery lists from recipes plus manual items
  • Saved recipes, lists, notes, ratings, and mark cooked
  • Meal calendar with optional one-way device calendar export
  • My Kitchen pantry staples
  • English, French, Italian, and Spanish

Credits, not a subscription

You get 1 free full recipe every day. New users also start with 2 credits. When you need more, buy packs of 6, 28, or 90 credits in the app. Prices are shown in the App Store or Google Play. Delixio does not require a subscription. Credits do not expire.
