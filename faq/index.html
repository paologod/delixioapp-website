About Delixio

What is Delixio?

Delixio is an AI recipe app that creates recipe ideas from ingredients you already have, then helps you unlock full recipes, save, shop, and plan.

Can Delixio help me decide what to cook?

Yes. Start from the ingredients you already have and generate meal ideas around them. A practical walkthrough is on what can I cook with these ingredients. For fridge-first cooking, see the fridge recipe app page.

How does Delixio work?

You type ingredients, set preferences (cooking time, servings, meal type, diet, dislikes), choose Strict / Flexible / Creative mode, and generate free recipe ideas. Unlock a full recipe when you want it (1 credit), then save recipes, build a grocery list, and plan meals. See also How it works.

What is the difference between recipe ideas and full recipes?

Recipe ideas are free to explore: you can generate them and select the ones you like. A full recipe is the complete version you unlock to cook (ingredients, steps, and tips or substitutions when provided). Unlocking each full recipe uses 1 credit. You can select several ideas and unlock them in one batch.

Is Delixio an AI recipe generator?

Yes. Delixio uses AI to generate free recipe ideas from the ingredients and preferences you provide, then unlock full recipes with credits. Recipes are AI-assisted suggestions, always check ingredients and allergens yourself before cooking. Read more about an AI recipe generator.

Who makes Delixio?

Delixio is published by ACCESS24 SARL-S (Luxembourg), trading as Delixio. Learn more on the About page.

Is Delixio available on iPhone?

Yes. Delixio is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone.

Is Delixio available on Android?

Yes. Delixio is available on Google Play for Android.

Which languages does Delixio support?

The app UI is available in English, French, Italian, and Spanish (plus your system default). Recipes follow the app language you choose.

Recipes & ingredients

How does Delixio create recipes from ingredients?

Type the ingredients you want to use, choose your preferences and cooking mode, and Delixio generates free personalized recipe ideas. Ideas show how many of your ingredients appear. Unlock a full recipe with 1 credit when you want the complete version. See also recipe generator from ingredients.

Do I have to enter every ingredient in my kitchen?

No. You only need to type the ingredients you want to cook with. Kitchen can keep items At home, staples you Always have, and optional Use first priorities. Delixio does not require a barcode scanner or fridge camera.

What is Kitchen?

Kitchen separates items that are At home from staples you Always have. You can mark At home ingredients to Use first when you want to prioritize them. Kitchen context helps generation and does not automatically create Grocery items.

Can I use leftovers?

Yes. Enter leftover ingredients and Delixio can suggest practical meal ideas that use them. More ideas: leftover recipe generator. Related approaches: use ingredients before they expire and reduce food waste.

Can Delixio suggest recipes when I'm missing ingredients?

Yes. Depending on the cooking mode, Delixio can suggest recipes that may need a few extra items. From a full recipe, you can add selected ingredients to your grocery list.

Can I exclude ingredients?

Yes. You can set disliked ingredients so generation treats them as hard excludes.

Can I choose the number of servings?

Yes. You can choose servings before generating ideas, and Delixio adjusts quantities for full recipes accordingly.

Can I choose how much time I have to cook?

Yes. You can choose cooking time bands: under 30 minutes, 30–60 minutes, or 1 hour+, and Delixio will suggest ideas that fit. For weeknight frameworks, see quick dinner ideas.

Can I choose a meal type?

Yes. You can set meal type to breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack before generating ideas.

Can Delixio suggest vegetarian, pescatarian, or vegan meals?

Yes. You can set diet preferences: none, vegetarian, pescatarian, or vegan, so Delixio suggests recipes that fit. Always review ingredients yourself before cooking.

Cooking modes

Strict, Flexible, and Creative modes let you control how closely recipes stick to the ingredients you already have.

What is Strict mode?

Strict mode stays with the ingredients you entered and your Kitchen context, favoring recipes that fit what you already have.

What is Flexible mode?

Flexible mode allows about 1–2 missing ingredients so you can discover recipes that need only minor additions.

What is Creative mode?

Creative mode explores more inventive recipe ideas and can suggest useful additions around the ingredients you have.

Which Delixio cooking mode should I use?

Use Strict when you want to cook only with what you have. Use Flexible when you are open to buying one or two extras. Use Creative when you want more inventive ideas with useful additions.

Saving & planning

Can I save recipes?

Yes. You can save recipes and organize them in lists, including Favorites, Quick dinners, Healthy, and custom lists you create. Your Recipes library can search title, notes, and ingredients, with filters, ratings, and cooked status to help you find meals again.

Can I create favorites?

Yes. You can mark recipes as favorites so your best discoveries are easy to find.

Can I rate recipes?

Yes. Delixio includes recipe ratings so you can keep track of meals you liked.

Can I add notes?

Yes. You can add private notes to recipes to remember tweaks, timing, or serving ideas.

Can I mark a recipe as cooked?

Yes. You can mark recipes as cooked to keep track of meals you have made.

Can Delixio create grocery lists?

Yes. From a recipe you can add selected ingredients to a grocery list, and you can also add items manually (optional quantity), check them off, and clear completed items. Learn more on the grocery list recipe app page.

Can I schedule a recipe?

Yes. You can add recipes to the meal calendar to schedule when you want to cook them. You can also plan a shopping day from your grocery list.

Does Delixio include meal planning?

Yes. Delixio includes Plan with Meals and Grocery for planning meals and shopping ahead. You can plan from saved recipes, review missing ingredients, add them to Grocery, and export plans one-way to your device calendar. Changes made only in Apple or Google Calendar are not synced back into Delixio. See the meal planner app overview.

Chef Journey

What is Chef Journey?

Chef Journey tracks lifetime full recipes you create and adds bonus recipe credits at milestones: 10 recipes (+1), 30 (+1), 50 (+2), and 100 (+4). Daily free full recipes count toward progress.

Where do Chef Journey rewards go?

Bonus credits go into your normal recipe-credit balance. Chef Journey is not a subscription tier, badge system, leaderboard, or separate wallet.

Credits & pricing

Is Delixio free?

Recipe ideas are free to generate. You also get 1 free full recipe every day. Extra full recipes use credits from optional packs. No subscription.

What is the daily free recipe credit?

Every calendar day you get 1 free full recipe at no credit cost. It is always used first when you unlock full recipes, and it resets at local midnight. Recipe ideas themselves are free to explore.

Does Delixio require a subscription?

No. Delixio uses optional credit packs rather than a recurring subscription.

How do recipe credits work?

1 credit unlocks 1 full recipe. Recipe ideas are free. New users start with 2 credits, plus 1 free full recipe every day. Optional packs are 6 (trying new ideas), 28 (weekly planning), or 90 (best value) via the App Store or Google Play. Prices are shown in the store in your local currency. If a full-recipe generation fails, no credit is used for that recipe.

Do purchased credits expire?

No. Credits never expire, so you can use them whenever you want. Buying packs does not remove your daily free full recipe.

Can I restore my purchases?

Yes. You can restore purchases in the app (through the App Store or Google Play) if credits do not appear after buying a pack.

Account & privacy

How do I sign in to Delixio?

You can sign in with email (passwordless one-time code), Google, or Apple (Apple sign-in on iOS).

Does Delixio send marketing push notifications?

No. Optional reminders are local notifications on your device (for example when your daily free full recipe is ready). They are off until you opt in in Settings. Meal planner alerts use your device calendar reminder when you export events, not Delixio marketing push.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can delete your account in the app, which removes your personal data. See also the delete account page and our Privacy Policy.

Does Delixio store my card number?

No. Purchases go through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Delixio does not store your card numbers.
