Information We Collect

Account Information

When creating an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers
  • Apple Sign-In account information
  • Google Sign-In account information

User Content

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Ingredients entered into the application
  • Recipe preferences
  • Saved recipes
  • Favorite recipes
  • Grocery lists
  • Meal planning information
  • Notes associated with recipes

Purchase Information

Purchases are processed by Apple and Google.

We do not receive full payment card details.

We may receive transaction information necessary to validate purchases and manage credits.

Analytics and Diagnostics

We may collect:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Crash reports
  • Usage statistics
  • Feature interaction data
  • Performance metrics

Analytics providers may include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • RevenueCat
  • Microsoft Clarity

AI Processing

Recipe generation requests are processed using OpenAI services.

Information submitted for recipe generation may be transmitted to OpenAI solely for the purpose of generating recipe suggestions.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide recipe generation services
  • Authenticate users
  • Manage accounts
  • Deliver purchased credits
  • Improve app performance
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Analyze feature usage
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal Basis (GDPR)

Where applicable, we process personal data on the basis of:

  • Contract performance
  • Legitimate interests
  • Legal obligations
  • User consent where required

Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that assist us in operating Delixio, including:

  • Supabase
  • OpenAI
  • Google Firebase
  • RevenueCat
  • Microsoft Clarity
  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play Store

We do not sell personal information.

International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required, appropriate safeguards will be implemented to protect personal data.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data

Requests may be submitted to:

support@delixioapp.com

Account Deletion

Users may delete their account directly within the Delixio application.

You may also follow the procedure specified on our Delete Account page.

Children's Privacy

Delixio is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updated versions will be published within the application and/or on our website.
