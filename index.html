FAQ

Questions about Delixio.

What is Delixio?

Delixio is an AI recipe app that creates recipe ideas from ingredients you already have, then helps you unlock full recipes, save, shop, and plan.

How does Delixio create recipes from my ingredients?

Type the ingredients you want to use, choose preferences and cooking mode, explore free recipe ideas, then unlock a full recipe when you want to cook.

Is Delixio free?

Recipe ideas are free. You also get 1 free full recipe every day. Extra full recipes use optional credit packs. No subscription.

Does Delixio require a subscription?

No. Delixio uses optional credit packs (6, 28, or 90) rather than a recurring subscription.

Is Delixio available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. Delixio is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Can Delixio create grocery lists and plan meals?

Yes. You can add ingredients to a grocery list, save recipes to lists, plan meals on the calendar, and optionally export plans one-way to your device calendar.
