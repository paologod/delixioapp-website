Dinner decisions

What can I cook with these ingredients?

When people ask “what can I cook with these ingredients?”, they usually have a short list: half a zucchini, some eggs, rice, a lonely chicken breast, or yesterday’s roast vegetables. The goal is not a perfect cookbook meal. The goal is a tasty dinner without another trip to the store.

Start by naming your constraints, time, servings, and how willing you are to buy missing items, then match your ingredients to a cooking format. Delixio can do this automatically once you enter what you have.

Ask four questions first

Those answers prevent endless scrolling through recipes that do not fit your fridge or your evening.

  • How much time do I have?
  • How many people am I cooking for?
  • What must be used soon (leftovers or produce that will not last)?
  • Am I cooking only with what I have, or can I add one small missing item?

Match ingredients to a meal format

Once you know your ingredients, pick a format that can absorb leftovers well:

Skillet / stir-fry

Best for mixed vegetables, leftover meat, eggs, tofu, or cooked rice.

Soup or stew

Best when you have odds and ends of vegetables, beans, or leftover roast.

Pasta or noodles

Best when you have a sauce base (tomatoes, cream, pesto, garlic-oil) plus a few add-ins.

Bowl or salad

Best for cooked grains, greens, beans, cheese, and leftover protein.

Eggs for dinner

Best when the fridge looks empty but you still have eggs, cheese, and a vegetable.

FAQ

What if I only have a few ingredients?

Focus on one format that stretches food: eggs, pasta, rice bowls, soups, and skillet meals are usually the most forgiving.

Should I include spices and oil in my list?

Yes if they matter for flavor. Even a short list of garlic, lemon, chili, soy sauce, or herbs can change the meal.

How does Delixio help with this question?

You enter the ingredients you have and Delixio generates personalized recipe ideas around them. You can unlock full recipes with credits and add missing ingredients to a grocery list.

Can I avoid ingredients I dislike?

Yes. In Delixio, disliked ingredients are treated as hard excludes during generation, and you can also set diet preferences.
