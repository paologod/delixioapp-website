About Delixio

Delixio is an AI recipe app that generates practical recipe ideas from the ingredients you already have at home.

You get 1 free full recipe every day. Recipe ideas are free to explore. When you need more full recipes, buy credit packs (6, 28, or 90) in the app, there is no subscription. Credits do not expire.

Save the recipes you want to make again, plan meals from them, and add only missing ingredients to Grocery. Chef Journey can also award bonus credits as you reach lifetime full-recipe milestones. Learn more about Delixio or see how it works.
