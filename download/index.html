About Delixio

Download Delixio for iPhone and Android. Delixio is an AI recipe app that generates practical recipe ideas from the ingredients you already have at home.

Every user gets one free daily recipe credit. When you need more, you can buy recipe credit packs inside the app — there is no subscription.

Delixio also includes a grocery list for missing ingredients and a meal calendar to save recipes and plan meals ahead.
