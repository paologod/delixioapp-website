Why no subscription matters for cooking apps

Cooking help should fit real kitchen habits. Some weeks you cook every night. Some weeks you barely open the fridge. A credit model gives you more control than a monthly plan if your cooking rhythm changes.

Delixio gives recipe ideas for free, includes one free full recipe every day, and offers optional credit packs for extra full recipes.

What to check before choosing a recipe app

Look for a tool that starts from your ingredients, not just from a recipe database.

Check whether it can save recipes, create a grocery list, and help plan meals on a calendar.

Avoid apps that make strong savings, health, or nutrition promises without evidence. A recipe app should help with ideas, not pretend to guarantee outcomes.
