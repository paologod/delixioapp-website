Frequently Asked Questions

I didn't receive my verification code

  • Check your spam folder.
  • Wait a few minutes and request a new code.

I purchased recipe credits but didn't receive them

  • Restart the app.
  • If the credits still don't appear after a few minutes, contact us with your purchase details.

Where can I see Chef Journey progress and bonus credits?

Open Home and select Chef Rewards. For how lifetime milestones and reward credits work, see the Chef Journey FAQ.

My bonus credit is not showing

Try closing and reopening the app. If the balance still does not update, contact support with the email on your Delixio account and a short description of what you reached.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on our Delete Account page, or contact us at support@delixioapp.com.
