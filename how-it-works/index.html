How it works

How Delixio works

Delixio turns ingredients you already have into personalized recipe ideas, then helps you save meals, build grocery lists, and plan what to cook next.

A real cooking flow

From the ingredients you have to a plan you can use

Every screen has a job: help you decide what to cook, keep the recipe worth returning to, and make the next shop simpler.

Start here

Open Delixio when dinner is still a question

Home keeps your next action close—start cooking, return to your Recipes, plan ahead, or check your Chef Journey progress.

01 — Cook from what is there

Add ingredients, then set the rules

Enter what you want to use, choose preferences, and decide whether to stay Strict, allow a few extras with Flexible, or explore with Creative.

02 — Explore, then cook

Browse ideas for free. Unlock the full recipe when it fits.

Compare practical recipe ideas first. When one is right for tonight, open the complete recipe, review what is missing, and choose to cook, plan, or add gaps to Grocery.

03 — Keep the context that helps

Make everyday cooking easier to pick up again

Kitchen separates what is At home from staples you Always have and ingredients to Use first. Recipes keeps the meals you want to find again, with search, lists, notes, ratings, and cooked status.

04 — Plan meals. Shop the gaps.

Turn a recipe decision into a realistic week

Plan from recipes you have saved, then add only the missing ingredients to Grocery. Keep a simple list, check items off while shopping, and use the meal calendar when you want to look ahead.

05 — Build your cooking momentum

Chef Journey adds a little extra as you cook

Lifetime full-recipe milestones can add bonus credits to your normal balance. It is progress for real cooking—not a subscription tier or a separate wallet.

The full flow, step by step

  1. 1. Enter the ingredients you have

    Type or add the ingredients you want to use. Kitchen keeps items that are At home, staples you Always have, and optional Use first priorities. No barcode scanner or fridge camera required. For a deeper look at ingredient-first cooking, see recipe generator from ingredients.

  2. 2. Choose preferences

    Set diet preferences, disliked ingredients, cooking time, servings, and meal type so suggestions fit how you actually cook. When time is short, the same flow supports quick dinner ideas based on what you already have.

  3. 3. Choose Strict, Flexible or Creative mode

    Strict stays with only what you entered. Flexible allows about 1–2 missing items. Creative explores more inventive ideas with smart extras when you need them.

  4. 4. Explore free recipe ideas

    Delixio generates practical meal ideas based on your ingredients, preferences, and selected mode, similar to what people expect from an AI recipe generator. Ideas are free to explore.

  5. 5. Unlock a full recipe

    When you want the complete recipe, unlocking it uses 1 credit. You get 1 free full recipe every day (used before paid credits). Extra packs are optional.

  6. 6. Review missing ingredients

    See which items you may still need so you can decide whether to cook now or adjust the recipe.

  7. 7. Save recipes

    Save recipes to lists (Favorites, Quick dinners, Healthy, or custom), add notes and ratings, and mark meals as cooked.

  8. 8. Build a grocery list

    Add ingredients from recipes to Grocery, or add items manually. Always have staples stay in Kitchen context and are not automatically added to Grocery. More on that workflow: grocery list recipe app.

  9. 9. Plan meals

    Add recipes to the meal calendar to plan dinners ahead. Optionally export plans one-way to your device calendar. See also the meal planner app page.

  10. 10. Credits without a subscription

    Use your free daily full recipe, or buy packs of 6, 28, or 90 credits when you want more. Credits do not expire. Delixio does not require a subscription.

  11. 11. Build your cooking momentum

    Chef Journey awards bonus recipe credits at lifetime full-recipe milestones. Daily free full recipes count, and rewards go into your normal credit balance.
